Simple first-run instructions

How to save Microsoft Teams captions

  1. Install Teams Caption Recorder. Add the browser extension from the Chrome Web Store. Open its one-time setup page.
  2. Choose consent. Read the privacy summary and click Enable automatic captions and saving. Automatic capture stays OFF until you opt in.
  3. Join a Teams meeting in Chrome or Edge. Use the web version at teams.microsoft.com or teams.cloud.microsoft. The native Teams desktop app is not supported.
  4. Keep the meeting natural. The extension quietly tries to enable Teams live captions and captures available caption text. You won't hear an announcement from the extension.
  5. Leave and export. Hang up normally. The transcript finishes and stays saved in your browser. Click the extension icon → Open saved transcripts → export TXT, MD, JSON, or WebVTT.

Teams creates the live captions; this tool does not perform speech recognition. It cannot recover speech from before captions were turned on. Follow meeting-consent rules before capturing participants' words.

Answers before you install

Frequently asked questions

Does this extension record meeting audio?

No. It reads the text already displayed by Microsoft Teams' live captions. It does not access microphone audio, generate speech, or start a native Teams recording.

Will participants see a recording announcement?

The extension adds no audio or text announcements to the meeting. Teams may still show its own caption interface or system notifications. Always follow your organization's rules and applicable law.

Can I retrieve captions from an older Teams meeting?

No. It only captures text rendered in a supported Teams browser meeting while the extension is enabled. It is not a historical transcript downloader.

Where are transcripts stored?

Locally in the browser profile's IndexedDB. Nothing is automatically uploaded to the developer. You can export transcripts manually, delete individual records, or clear all extension storage.

Does it work with the Teams desktop application?

No. Use Microsoft Teams in Chrome or Edge. Captions are only captured when Teams renders them in a supported web page.
