How to save Microsoft Teams live captions to a text transcript

Teams live captions help you follow a meeting as it happens. To keep the caption text afterward, use a browser extension that saves the captions Teams displays. This guide covers automatic caption activation, speaker labels, local storage and transcript exports.

Before recording: Obtain permission to retain participants' words and follow your organization's rules and applicable laws. This tool does not record audio or video.

Save Teams captions in six steps

  1. Install the free Chrome extension. Open Teams Caption Recorder – Local Notes in Chrome Web Store and select Add to Chrome.
  2. Choose your recording preference. Read the one-time privacy explanation. Automatic capture and caption enablement start OFF on a new installation; choose Enable automatic captions and saving if you wish to opt in.
  3. Join a Teams meeting in your browser. Sign in to Microsoft Teams at teams.microsoft.com or teams.cloud.microsoft. The extension does not capture the native Windows or macOS Teams application.
  4. Let Teams display live captions. With automation enabled, the extension tries to open Teams' built-in caption controls after you join. If the captions do not appear, use Teams' More → Language and speech → Show live captions menu or the extension's Retry live captions control.
  5. Let participants speak. The extension saves the text that appears in the Teams caption pane, including the speaker name when Teams provides one and an approximate browser timestamp. It does not recover speech from before captions were active.
  6. Leave, then export. Leave the call normally. After the transcript is saved, click the extension toolbar icon → Open saved transcripts, select the meeting, and choose the export format you need.

What export format should I choose?

TXT

Plain-text Teams caption transcript for copying into notes or email.

Markdown

Readable meeting notes with structure that works in many editors.

JSON

Structured caption segments, speaker labels and observation times for your own analysis.

WebVTT

A caption-oriented text format useful for compatible subtitle workflows. Timestamps are approximate and not derived from recorded audio.

Are live captions the same as a Teams transcript?

No. Microsoft Teams creates live captions for viewing during a call. Organizations may separately enable Teams' own meeting transcription features, which have different requirements and controls. Teams Caption Recorder does not request a native Teams recording or a Microsoft-hosted transcript. It saves only the live caption text actually rendered in a supported Teams browser page.

Can I save captions without recording meeting audio?

Yes. The extension observes Teams' caption text and stores it locally in your browser profile's IndexedDB. It has no microphone recording, speech playback, in-meeting recording banner or automatic transcript upload to the publisher. Teams may still show its own built-in caption interface and status messages. Read the privacy policy for details on retention, deletion and exports.

Why are some captions missing or repeated?

Teams sends partial caption revisions as people speak. The extension reconciles many of those changes, but unexpected changes in Teams' interface, tabs or meeting controls may still interrupt or divide a recording. Keep the Teams meeting tab open, check that captions are appearing during a test, and review exported transcripts for continuity. The extension cannot capture text that Teams never displays.

What if automatic Teams captions do not turn on?

Confirm that you opted in, enabled Automatically turn on captions in the Chrome toolbar popup, and joined a live meeting in a supported web browser. If necessary, use the Teams menu manually and check the displayed language. See the full troubleshooting instructions for additional cases.

Can I use this with the Teams desktop app?

No. The extension works in Chrome and compatible Chromium browsers with the supported Teams web experience. It does not inspect a separate native Teams application, the computer microphone, or previously recorded meeting files.

What should I do before uninstalling?

Export any meeting transcripts you want to keep. The extension's locally saved sessions may be deleted when its storage or browser profile is removed. An unpacked developer installation and a Chrome Web Store installation can use different extension IDs, so their saved transcripts will not automatically transfer.
